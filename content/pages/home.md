PE502 is a research site about **castles**: polyominoes made of columns standing side by side on a common base, with at least one column reaching a fixed ceiling. It takes its name from Project Euler problem 502, which asks how many castles on a large grid are built from an even number of blocks.

Two kinds of things are posted here. Posts are short, dated updates, newest first below. [Publications](/publications/) are standalone documents with permanent numbers: PE502-1, PE502-2, and so on. Both are sorted by subject into [divisions](/divisions/).
