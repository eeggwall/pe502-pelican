Title: Even-block castles on a 13 × 10 grid
Date: 2026-10-01
Slug: even-block-castles-on-a-13-by-10-grid
Tags: E Division

There are 3,729,050,610,636 castles on a 13 &times; 10 grid with an even number of blocks, about 2<sup>41.8</sup>. Listing them one at a time stops being an option long before the sizes Project Euler asks about, which reach 10<sup>12</sup> in one dimension.

[PE502-1](/publications/PE502-1/) weights each castle by a sign instead, and reduces the even-block count to a signed tower count whose generating function is rational.
