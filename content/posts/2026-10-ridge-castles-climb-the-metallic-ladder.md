Title: Ridge castles climb the metallic ladder
Date: 2026-10-04
Slug: ridge-castles-climb-the-metallic-ladder
Tags: M Division, N Division

In a ridge castle, neighboring columns differ in height unless both reach the ceiling. At height 2 ridge castles grow like &phi;<sup>*w*</sup>, with &phi; the golden ratio. At height *h* they grow like &delta;<sub>*h*&minus;1</sub><sup>*w*</sup>, where &delta;<sub>*a*</sub> = (*a*&nbsp;+&nbsp;&radic;(*a*<sup>2</sup>&nbsp;+&nbsp;4))/2 is the metallic mean of order *a*: golden at height 2, silver (1 + &radic;2) at height 3, bronze ((3&nbsp;+&nbsp;&radic;13)/2) at height 4.

Section 6 of [PE502-2](/publications/PE502-2/) has the transfer matrix and the counts.
